Research for issue #4894, ask 3 — service principal synchronization jobs. Checked 2026-11-18 against AzureAD `main` commit [`e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882`](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/tree/e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882) and this worktree at `d206ea3ad65de70cd1852eb622070189db0f893d`. Research only: no tenant requests, acceptance tests, provisioning starts, resource implementation, commits, or pushes. Findings are synthesized; no MPL implementation was copied.

**Recommended first resource contract.** Use the beta client and direct Graph calls in CRUD, with this provider's shared timeout, conversion, schema, error and read-consistency helpers. Required replacement fields: bare UUID `service_principal_id` and nonempty connector-specific `template_id`. Export opaque Graph `job_id` separately from composite Terraform `id`; use `{service_principal_id}/{job_id}` for consistency with the sibling schema resource. Expose computed schedule and useful status; keep settings computed until their write contract is established. Prefer disabled creation by default, with explicit `enabled` opt-in. These are design recommendations, not claims of AzureAD compatibility.

**What AzureAD actually implements.** Both parent and template are immutable. `enabled` defaults to true; schedule is computed. Create checks the parent under a per-service-principal lock, posts `templateId`, waits through GET 404s, records its ID, optionally starts, then reads. Update only starts/pauses when `enabled` changes. Read derives enabled from `schedule.state == Active`; a GET 404 removes state. Delete locks the parent, deletes, then waits for GET 404. It does not configure job settings, schema, schedule, or credentials. Defaults are 15 minutes create and 5 minutes otherwise. Avoid reproducing its unchecked nil schedule and its lack of special handling for an initial DELETE 404. [Pinned schema/CRUD](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/blob/e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882/internal/services/synchronization/synchronization_job_resource.go#L27-L266).

The upstream SDK accepts additional success statuses for create/start/pause, but that is not evidence that a response without a job ID can satisfy Terraform Create. [Pinned SDK create contract](https://github.com/hashicorp/go-azure-sdk/blob/7eea1802e3aff42b0c251ae480f5c86fb02cb2a7/microsoft-graph/serviceprincipals/stable/synchronizationjob/method_createsynchronizationjob.go#L53-L65).

**Template and parent dependencies.** Synchronization template IDs are text such as `dataBricks`, `scim`, `BoxOutDelta` or `Slack`; they are not application-template GUIDs. Discover them from the target parent's `/synchronization/templates`, retaining exact spelling. A template supplies connector behavior and a default schema; multiple templates may exist and application-specific guidance determines the appropriate one. Do not accept a gallery template GUID as interchangeable with a synchronization template ID. [Synchronization template](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/synchronization-synchronizationtemplate?view=graph-rest-beta), [template discovery](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/synchronization-synchronization-list-templates?view=graph-rest-beta).

A plain service principal does not prove that the desired provisioning connector/template exists. AzureAD's example instantiates the Databricks gallery application, obtains its service principal, configures separate secrets, and creates the `dataBricks` job. Its example does not express a dependency from the job to the secret; examples here must explicitly order prerequisites when enabling. [Pinned AzureAD example](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/blob/e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882/docs/resources/synchronization_job.md#L19-L50). Graph beta also supports instantiating a non-gallery Custom application using global template GUID `8adf8e6e-67b2-4cf2-a259-e3dc5476c621`; instantiate returns the application/service-principal pair. National clouds have different Custom template IDs. [Beta instantiate](https://learn.microsoft.com/graph/api/applicationtemplate-instantiate?preserve-view=true&view=graph-rest-beta).

**Schedule, status, and settings.** Graph explicitly marks the job's `schedule` read-only and does not list an Update method. Keep expiration, ISO 8601 interval and state computed; an SDK setter or generated PATCH is not proof that Graph accepts a writable schedule. Schedule states are `Active`, `Disabled`, and `Paused`. [Job model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/synchronization-synchronizationjob?view=graph-rest-beta), [schedule model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/synchronization-synchronizationschedule?view=graph-rest-beta).

Job status is independent of schedule: `NotConfigured`, `NotRun`, `Active`, `Paused`, or `Quarantine`, with execution, failure and quarantine diagnostics. A newly created job with `NotConfigured` is valid; do not wait for a successful provisioning execution as Create completion. Do not treat quarantine as a reason to restart during Read. [Status model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/resources/synchronization-synchronizationstatus?view=graph-rest-beta). `synchronizationJobSettings` may inherit template values; the pinned local beta SDK models it as `[]KeyValuePairable` despite the documentation's singular JSON illustration. No documented writable settings contract was established; computed outputs avoid overwriting service defaults. [SDK job getters](https://github.com/microsoftgraph/msgraph-beta-sdk-go/blob/v0.168.0/models/synchronization_job.go#L143-L206).

**IDs and import history.** Current AzureAD `service_principal_id` is a scoped `/servicePrincipals/{uuid}` string; its resource ID/import is `/servicePrincipals/{uuid}/synchronization/jobs/{opaqueJobId}`. Both variable segments are generic strings in its parser, not UUID validators. [Pinned parent parser](https://github.com/hashicorp/go-azure-sdk/blob/7eea1802e3aff42b0c251ae480f5c86fb02cb2a7/microsoft-graph/common-types/stable/id_serviceprincipal.go#L85-L96), [job parser](https://github.com/hashicorp/go-azure-sdk/blob/7eea1802e3aff42b0c251ae480f5c86fb02cb2a7/microsoft-graph/common-types/stable/id_serviceprincipalidsynchronizationjob.go#L91-L105). Historical AzureAD state used `{uuid}/job/{jobId}`, now upgraded to the scoped form. A September 2024 fix also changed parent parsing/readback from bare UUID to scoped ID; import confusion persisted in issue #1658. [Migration](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/blob/e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882/internal/services/synchronization/migrations/synchronization_job_resource.go#L63-L72), [parent parsing fix](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/commit/e1c1777d49915456d7fe1a3ba91899ffb3237660), [import issue](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/issues/1658).

For this provider, retain a bare parent UUID and opaque job string; validate import structure, parent UUID, and nonempty job ID without `/`. Do not lower-case, split on dots, infer the job ID, or apply a UUID validator to it. Export `job_id` so schema configuration consumes `job.job_id`, not the composite `job.id`. The sibling already defines the `{uuid}/{jobId}` import convention and replacement behavior. [Sibling schema](https://github.com/deploymenttheory/terraform-provider-microsoft365/blob/d206ea3ad65de70cd1852eb622070189db0f893d/internal/services/resources/applications/graph_beta/service_principal_synchronization_schema_json/resource.go#L75-L143).

**Idempotency and propagation recommendations.** No documented natural-key uniqueness, duplicate-POST behavior or idempotency guarantee was found. The upstream creates directly, without adopting/listing an existing same-template job. Therefore import existing jobs explicitly; do not silently claim them. Persist a returned job ID before polling or starting so a later error retains recoverable ownership. After an ambiguous POST/network failure, reconcile with GET/list and report candidates rather than blindly assuming a repeat POST is harmless. Do not synthesize IDs from template or tenant IDs. [Create contract](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/synchronization-synchronization-post-jobs?view=graph-rest-beta), [list contract](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/synchronization-synchronization-list-jobs?view=graph-rest-beta).

Use bounded GET polling after create and start/pause, checking actual schedule transitions rather than accepting the first stale 200. Ordinary refresh 404 means missing job or missing parent; remove state. A post-create 404 is propagation and must preserve state while retrying; DELETE 404 means already absent. Do not turn unrelated 400/401/403 responses into successful absence. This provider's shared error handler defaults to dropping ordinary Read state on 400 as well as 404; use its `PreserveStateOnReadBadRequest` option for this endpoint. Preserve `retry_operation` when using `crud.ReadWithRetry`, and use its `ConsistencyPredicate` for schedule readback. [Shared retry](https://github.com/deploymenttheory/terraform-provider-microsoft365/blob/d206ea3ad65de70cd1852eb622070189db0f893d/internal/services/common/crud/read_with_retry.go#L18-L45), [shared error handling](https://github.com/deploymenttheory/terraform-provider-microsoft365/blob/d206ea3ad65de70cd1852eb622070189db0f893d/internal/services/common/errors/kiota/error_handling.go#L79-L181).

AzureAD retries 409 and 403 indiscriminately; use this only as evidence of observed upstream consistency handling, not proof that all permission failures are transient. [Retry helper](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/blob/e4bf321593a89f1363ef6ebe457f5843b8046882/internal/services/synchronization/synchronization.go#L19-L23). Real 401 failures were resolved by correcting delegated-versus-application permissions or granting synchronization application permissions to the calling identity. [Issue #1099 resolution](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/issues/1099#issuecomment-1544646974), [issue #1628 resolution](https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform-provider-azuread/issues/1628#issuecomment-2607568872). Current beta docs specify `Application.ReadWrite.OwnedBy` or `Synchronization.ReadWrite.All` for writes, and ownership-scoped access or synchronization read/write permissions for reads; use these over AzureAD's older broad permission prose. [Create permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/synchronization-synchronization-post-jobs?view=graph-rest-beta), [read permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/synchronization-synchronizationjob-get?view=graph-rest-beta).

**Interoperation and safe verification.** A job configured `enabled=true` can start before its separately managed schema child is applied. Referencing `job.job_id` creates a dependency from schema to job, so adding the reverse dependency creates a cycle. Document staged disabled creation, schema/credential configuration, then explicit enabling. Destroying the sibling resets the schema while retaining the job; destroying this resource deletes the job and its state. [Sibling lifecycle](https://github.com/deploymenttheory/terraform-provider-microsoft365/blob/d206ea3ad65de70cd1852eb622070189db0f893d/internal/services/resources/applications/graph_beta/service_principal_synchronization_schema_json/crud.go#L15-L249).
